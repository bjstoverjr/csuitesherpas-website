Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

C Suite Sherpas LLC ("C Suite Sherpas," "we," "us") runs csuitesherpas.com. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you fill out the contact form, we receive your name, work email, company, role, the topic you chose and your message. If you email or call us, we receive what you share.
  • Basic site usage. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies, and Google Analytics, which uses cookies to tell us how visitors find and use the site (for example, pages viewed, how you arrived, and general location and device type). We do not use this to identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, schedule a call and prepare a proposal.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve the site.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and our email: Cloudflare (hosting, security and analytics), Google Analytics (site usage measurement), a form-delivery service that forwards contact-form messages to our inbox, and Google Workspace (email and calendar). They process information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep contact-form messages and related emails for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a business record, generally no longer than three years unless we are working together.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you sent us. Email bstover@csuitesherpas.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and access to our inbox is limited to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive personal or financial details through the form.

Children

This site is for business audiences and is not directed to children under 13.

Links to other sites

The site links to technology vendors and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own; please review their policies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
