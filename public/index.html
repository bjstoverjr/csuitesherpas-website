About

Billy J. Stover Jr., founder

BJ has spent 25+ years at the intersection of enterprise technology and the problems that show up on income statements: as an operator running call centers and training teams, as a buyer evaluating vendors and defending budgets, and as the seller and advisor shaping the deal. He has led sales and marketing at Universal Music Publishing Group, Viamedia and MultiView, and consulted on CX, contact-center and AI programs for Fortune 500 companies.

Since founding C Suite Sherpas in 2020, he has also worked as a contract enterprise consultant for Qualtrics and Genesys, helping leadership teams adopt cloud contact-center, workforce and AI automation. Across his career he has generated or influenced $35M+ in revenue, including $7.5M in IT services, and he stays hands-on from the first call through roll-out.
